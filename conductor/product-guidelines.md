# Product Guidelines: gh-domino

## Tone and Voice
The tone and voice of `gh-domino` should be technical, authoritative, concise, and direct. This approach ensures that communication is clear, efficient, and resonates with the target developer audience.

## Visual Identity
The visual identity of `gh-domino` is characterized by a clean, minimalist, and CLI-native aesthetic. The interface should be uncluttered, with a strong emphasis on clear, text-based output that integrates seamlessly into a terminal environment.

## Documentation
Documentation for `gh-domino` is considered helpful but not critical. The focus should be on providing enough information for users to get started and understand the core functionality. While extensive documentation is not a primary goal, the provided information should be accurate and easy to understand.
